## 1. Brand Essence

SK ZIC is SK Enmove's representative lubricant brand and evolving **fluid solution brand** — created to reduce friction, protect machines, maintain efficient movement, and deliver stable performance across changing operating conditions.

### Core Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Flow** | ZIC manages movement. Lubricant must flow, circulate, separate surfaces, transfer heat, carry contaminants, and remain functional. |
| **Protection** | ZIC protects engines, transmissions, and mechanical systems by reducing friction/wear, strengthening the lubricating film, controlling oxidation, preventing corrosion. |
| **Efficiency** | ZIC's fluid engineering reduces unnecessary mechanical and energy losses by controlling viscosity, friction, fluidity, and lubrication performance. |
| **Engineering** | ZIC's value is rooted in technical capability — VHVI Technology, YUBASE, additive formulation, viscosity control, OEM standards, laboratory testing. |
| **Progress** | ZIC is expanding beyond traditional engine oil toward a broader fluid-solution role spanning ICE, hybrid, EV, transmission, commercial, industrial, marine, hydraulic. |

### Official 2026 Brand Slogan: "Dynamics in Flow"
### Enduring Brand Role: "Fluid Solution"
### Strategic Direction: "Creating New Flow"

| Technical | Roboto Mono Medium/SemiBold | SAE grades, API/ACEA, OEM specs, test data, codes |

### Rules
- Display copy: concise and high-confidence
- Long paragraphs: Inter, not display type
- Technical specifications: always structured, not decorative
- Viscosity grades: visually prominent when product selection is involved
- Uppercase: selective, signals technical hierarchy, not shouting
- AVOID: stencil fonts, racing italics, sci-fi displays, pseudo-digital typefaces

## 5. Visual Principles — 12 Core Rules

1. **Flow Is the Brand Behaviour** — Every major visual should suggest movement, circulation, continuity, adaptation.
2. **Reveal the Invisible** — Show what conventional automotive photography cannot: oil film, friction interfaces, temperature, flow, pressure, wear, deposits, gears, internal motion.
3. **Protection Is Separation** — Lubrication prevents damaging contact by creating a protective film between moving surfaces.
4. **Technology Creates Authority** — YUBASE, VHVI, viscosity, additive functions, OEM specifications are legitimate visual content.
5. **Real Conditions Over Racing Fantasy** — Everyday and demanding driving conditions over generic motorsport theatre.
6. **Continuous Performance Over Momentary Power** — Stable function, consistent protection, controlled motion over one explosive moment.
7. **Darkness Must Reveal Something** — Carbon Black is justified when it helps reveal fluid, light, metal, mechanical geometry, heat, or product form.
8. **Data Is Proof** — Technical specs, property tables, standards, test results are part of the identity.
9. **Red Is Active** — ZIC Red marks energy, flow, interaction, movement, or important proof.
10. **Localise Context, Not Identity** — South African adaptations must still look globally ZIC.
11. **Product Claims Are Product-Specific** — Never transfer one oil's test data or OEM approval onto the whole portfolio.
12. **Specification Comes Before Recommendation** — Vehicle manufacturer's required specification first.

### Signature Device — "The Protection Layer"
MECHANICAL SURFACE → ZIC FLUID/OIL FILM → MECHANICAL SURFACE

### Product Selection Rule
> First identify the correct specification required by the vehicle manufacturer. Then match it to the relevant ZIC product.

Never recommend oil only by vehicle age, appearance, driving style, or generic "best oil" logic.

### Technical Claim Taxonomy (NOT interchangeable)
- Approved by — OEM tested and formally certified
- Certified by — Independent body verified compliance
- Meets — Product meets the standard (self-declared)
- Meets or exceeds — Performance equals or surpasses
- Suitable for use — Compatible but not formally approved
- Recommended for — Manufacturer suggests but does not mandate
- Designed for — Formulated with the application in mind

## 8. Technology & Proof Architecture

### YUBASE (Layer 01)
Premium Group III base oil. NOT an additive — it is the base-oil foundation.
Principle: "Performance begins with the base."

### VHVI Technology (Layer 02)
Very High Viscosity Index. Connected to YUBASE. Minimizes unwanted viscosity change across temperature.
Principle: "Stable under change."

### Additive Formulation (Layer 03)
Materials blended with base oils to enhance performance: viscosity-index improvement, dispersancy, detergent, antioxidant, anti-wear, anti-foam, friction modification, pour-point improvement.
Principle: "Base oil builds the foundation. Additives tune the function."

### Lubrication Functions (Layer 04)
1. Reduce friction and wear
2. Cooling / heat removal
3. Cleaning / contaminant control
4. Rust and corrosion protection
5. Sealing
6. Dispersing mechanical forces

### Lubrication States
- Fluid-film lubrication
- Boundary lubrication
- Extreme-pressure lubrication
